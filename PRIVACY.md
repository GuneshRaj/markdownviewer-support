# Privacy Policy — Markdown Viewer

**Effective date:** September 15, 2026

Markdown Viewer is built to be private by design.

## Data collection

Markdown Viewer collects **no data**. None.

- No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
- No accounts, sign-ins, or identifiers
- No advertising and no third-party SDKs
- Nothing you write, open, or export ever leaves your Mac

## How your documents are handled

- Documents are opened, edited, and rendered entirely on your device.
  Rendering (markdown, math, diagrams) uses libraries bundled inside the app
  and requires no network access.
- The **Chat with AI** feature uses Apple's on-device FoundationModels
  framework (Apple Intelligence). Your documents and questions are processed
  on your Mac and are never sent to us or to any third-party service.
- **Dictation** uses Apple's Speech framework. Depending on your macOS
  settings, Apple may process speech as described in Apple's own privacy
  policy; Markdown Viewer itself stores and transmits nothing.
- Files you open remain where they are. The app remembers recently opened
  file paths and folder-access permissions locally on your Mac only, so the
  app works as you expect between launches.

## Network access

The app's renderer never makes network requests. The only network activity
possible is fetching remote images that *you* reference in your own markdown
(e.g. `![](https://example.com/image.png)`) and links you choose to open in
your browser.
